# HDB lease and resale charts — methodology

Source: user-supplied “transactions.csv”; not independently reconciled with a live official dataset. 242,259 records, 11 original columns, no blank fields. Date range 2017-01–2026-10. CSV SHA-256: 5bed23d4e916abfe1d8c7f1857a674a24c2e8a400dbc2ff5a5c7c27dde9b0be5. transactions.csv is byte-identical to the upload. No deduplication, synthetic observations, trimming, or replacement of values. All records remain searchable in index.html, including rare flat types.

Remaining lease = stated years + stated months / 12; absent month component means zero months. S$/m² = resale_price / floor_area_sqm for each sale, then median; not ratio of aggregate medians. Bands are [0,50), [50,60), [60,70), [70,80), [80,100). Medians and quartiles use pandas linear quantile interpolation. F15 uses Tukey 1.5×IQR whiskers and plots every outlier. Distinct transactions may have coincident marks. All statistics are nominal SGD. Annual data are not repeat-sale observations or inflation-adjusted index values.

## Candidate audit and template provenance

The full catalog was evaluated first for Lupi Editorial, then Basics. L1 birth/current-scale fan, L6 network, L7 bipolar scales, L10 time-of-day, L11 event life histories, L13 funnel, L14 composition and L15 ballot percentages do not match price/lease data. L5/L12 record lists cannot legibly hold 242,259 items; L2's unit cascade does not encode continuous transaction prices. L3 is a short daily barcode, not suitable for nearly ten years of monthly transactions. L4 fits a small matrix, but L9 offers year rows and editorial marginalia for the lease mix. L8 decorative grids add no explanatory dimension. No Glance fallback is needed.

1. **F2 Hairline Line**, basics-gallery.html, “Thirty days of sign-ups”: calendar floor, thin polyline and period dots preserved; ten annual rather than daily summaries. Compared L3 (too many periods if using monthly data), F3 (area would overemphasize accumulated volume), F2 (explicit period medians). Annual aggregation is stated, not disguised as individual transactions.
2. **F5 Tick Rows**, basics-gallery.html, “Six teams, shipped and counted”: horizontal tick queues, guide lines, every-fifth dots. Compared L2 (vertical names and integer-unit composition less suitable), F1 (labels and exact median annotations more crowded), F5 (horizontal lease labels fit). One tick is S$100/m²; final tick is fractional, no rounding of the encoded median.
3. **F15 Tick Box**, basics-gallery.html, “Reply times, boxed by plan”: rounded IQR boxes, hairline whiskers, paper median, hollow outliers. Direct five-number-summary exception applies. F8 scatter is capped at 20 entities; F5 medians omit overlap; L19 density needs smoothing assumptions and is a reserve template. This is a statistical summary, not one point per input row.
4. **F6 Paired Rungs**, basics-gallery.html, “This year against last, plan by plan”: paired ladders, light/black series and zero baseline. Compared F12 (good for a few before/after endpoints but less natural for monetary rung units), F2 (would duplicate the temporal overview), F6 (distinct price/type comparison). One rung = S$25,000, final rung fractional. The common-type chart alone excludes rare 1-room and multi-generation groups; no raw records are removed.
5. **L9 Bubble Almanac**, lupi-gallery.html, “Eight years of tickets, one almanac”: ledger paper, year rows, seeded blob marks, side notes; radius proportional to square root of count, with fixed maximum radius. Compared L4 (little room for year context), F10 (week/hour contract wrong), L9 (year/category matrix matches). Original beta outline becomes a clearly labelled partial-year outline. Decorative randomness never alters values or positions.

All chart structures derive from these actual gallery rendering blocks. Dimensions and labels are adapted for English article inserts. MONO tokens are inlined; monochrome was chosen because a shared extra color dimension would add no stable information. Charts work offline with a system sans-serif fallback; Inter loads when network is available. Hover shows exact summary counts; chart clicks replay reveal. Reduced-motion CSS is included. The ledger loads on demand to avoid 242,259 DOM rows. It retains each original row and supports search/pagination. Five individual HTML charts accompany the combined chart page.

## Article interpretation

Market appreciation can coexist with lease ageing. Long-lease sales have higher unadjusted median S$/m² in 2025, but below 80 years the medians are not monotonic. Large within-band spread makes a single depreciation percentage misleading. Estimates of causal lease effects would require a separate controlled model and stronger identification; no such rate is inferred here. Remaining lease is measured at transaction time. Registration month is not necessarily contract date. No unique flat ID is supplied, so repeat-sale tracking is not assumed.
